        PRIVACY
        
Your data stays on this device

        
Drugcopeia has no account, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no telemetry. Nothing you search for, read or save is sent to us or to anyone else.

      

      

        

          
What we collect

          
Nothing.

          
    
            
  • No names, phone numbers, national IDs, email addresses or patient details.
    • 
            
  • No search history, reading history or reminder contents leave the app.
    • 
            
  • No device identifier, advertising ID, location or contacts. The camera and microphone open only from the Scan code and Voice search buttons, on your tap.
    • 
            
  • All processing happens on this device, including search, conditions, lab tests, dose checks, interactions, reminders and barcode decoding. Voice search hands the microphone audio to the device's speech service, set to on-device recognition where the system supports it.
    • 
            
  • Anything you type is checked on this device, and phone numbers, national IDs and email addresses are removed before the app uses them.
    • 
          

        


        

          
What can be downloaded

          
The medicine, condition and lab-test databases are built into the app. The only optional downloads are the news list and the photographs in the “Photos” section of a medicine page, and each one runs only when you press its own button, with no identifiers, cookies or tracking parameters.

          
    
          
    Every other network request is blocked inside the app. Links open in a browser view only when you tap them.
    
          
    Links — news, sources, product photographs — open in a browser view over the page you were reading. Drugcopeia reads nothing from it: no cookies, no history, no page content. Offline mode closes every link.
    
          
    On a phone that view is the browser component already on your device, with its own cookies and extensions, and it carries a button that hands the address to your default browser. On a computer Drugcopeia opens its own window and forgets it when you close it.
    
          
    Feedback and new-medicine notes are prepared inside the app and handed to your own email app. Nothing is sent until you press Send there, and no account or email address is required.
    
        
    

        
    
          
    Permissions and why
    
          
      
            
    • Notifications: to show the reminders you created, at the time you chose.
      • 
            
    • Alarms and restart (Android): to keep your reminders exact and restore them after a restart.
      • 
            
    • Internet: for the news update you press and the source links you open. The medicine directory works fully without it.
      • 
            
    • Camera: only when you scan a box code or take a search photo. A scanned frame is decoded on this device and is never stored or uploaded; a photo you pick for a search is sent to Google image search and Drugcopeia keeps no copy of it.
      • 
            
    • Microphone: only while Voice search is listening. Drugcopeia does not store the audio; the device’s speech service turns it into text, on-device where supported.
      • 
            
    • No location, contacts, phone state, files or advertising permissions are requested.
      • 
          
    
        
    

        
    
          
    Data on this device
    
          
    Reminders, medicines you add and the saved headlines stay in this app’s private storage. They are never uploaded, and this device’s cloud backup is disabled for Drugcopeia where the platform allows it.
